Aditya Raikwar

I build immersive systems that understand the people using them.I turn gaze, brain and behavior data into models that predict how people perform.I build AR and VR systems in Unity, then measure what they do to people.

I design AR and VR experiences in Unity, instrument them with eye-tracking and EEG, and train models that predict how people think and perform inside them.End-to-end Python pipelines for physiological and interaction data, from hybrid CNN-GRU networks to fine-tuned, personalized LLMs for human-in-the-loop systems.Unity (C#) environments that stream EEG and eye-tracking through Lab Streaming Layer to measure cognitive load and engagement, backed by commercial R&D funding.
